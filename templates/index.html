ABOUT RESUMEIQ

Built for smarter career decisions.

ResumeIQ is an AI-powered resume analysis platform built with Python, Flask, NLP and Scikit-learn.

Instead of simply checking whether keywords exist, the system evaluates resume structure, technical skills, ATS compatibility, job relevance and semantic similarity.

The result is a comprehensive report that helps candidates understand their strengths, identify gaps and make their resume more aligned with their target career.
